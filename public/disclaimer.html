Centerpost Medical & Crisis Disclaimer

Version: 1.0 Effective date: August 7, 2026 Contact: medicjth@gmail.com

🚨 If you are in crisis right now

Centerpost cannot help you in an emergency. It is not monitored. No one is reading your entries.

If you are thinking about harming yourself, or you are in immediate danger:

United States

Call or text 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Free, confidential, 24/7. Call 911 for a medical emergency. Text HOME to 741741 — Crisis Text Line.

United Kingdom & Ireland

Call 116 123 — Samaritans. Free, 24/7.

Canada

Call or text 988 — Suicide Crisis Helpline.

Australia

Call 13 11 14 — Lifeline.

Anywhere else

https://findahelpline.com — verified helplines in over 130 countries. Or go to your nearest emergency department.

Please close the app and reach out to a real person. That matters far more than anything in here.

1. What Centerpost is

Centerpost is a general wellness and productivity tool. It helps you organise tasks, plan your day, build routines, and use simple self-management techniques.

It is designed for everyday self-management — the kind of thing a planner, a journal, and a breathing exercise do. That is genuinely useful. It is also genuinely limited.

2. What Centerpost is not

Centerpost does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, therapy, or psychotherapy.

Specifically, Centerpost is not:

  • A medical device
  • A therapy or counselling service
  • A treatment programme for any condition, including ADHD, anxiety, depression, or any substance-use or behavioural condition
  • A diagnostic tool of any kind
  • A crisis service, a monitoring service, or an emergency service
  • A substitute for care from a licensed physician, psychiatrist, psychologist, therapist, counsellor, or other qualified professional
  • A replacement for medication or any treatment plan

Using Centerpost creates no clinician–patient, therapist–client, or professional relationship of any kind. Nobody at Centerpost LLC is acting as your healthcare provider.

3. About the wellness features

Some Centerpost features draw on ideas from evidence-based approaches, including cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) and dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT) — for example: paced breathing, grounding exercises, temperature and paced-breathing distress-tolerance techniques, HALT-style check-ins, urge-delay timers, and journaling.

These are offered as educational, self-directed skill tools. They are not clinical treatment.

The difference matters:

A licensed therapist provides Centerpost provides
Assessment of your specific situation A tool you choose to use
A treatment plan tailored to you The same generic exercises for everyone
Clinical judgement about what will help you No judgement or assessment at all
Monitoring, and adjustment when something isn't working No monitoring whatsoever
Accountability and a professional duty of care No duty of care
Crisis response Nothing

Using a breathing exercise in an app is not the same as doing DBT with a trained clinician. If these techniques are useful to you, that's great — and it is still worth talking to a professional about whether structured treatment would help more.

The Urge Log specifically is a tool for putting a deliberate pause between an impulse and an action, for everyday impulses like spending, phone use, snacking, or avoiding a task. It is not an addiction treatment tool, a relapse-prevention programme, or a substance-use intervention, and it should not be used as one. If you are managing a substance-use or behavioural addiction, please work with a qualified professional. In the US, SAMHSA's free confidential helpline is 1-800-662-4357, 24/7.

4. FDA status

Centerpost has not been evaluated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. It is not FDA cleared or approved.

Centerpost is intended to be a low-risk general wellness product as described in FDA guidance (General Wellness: Policy for Low Risk Devices): it promotes general relaxation, stress management, healthy habits, and self-organisation. It does not claim to diagnose, cure, mitigate, prevent, or treat any disease or condition.

Nothing in Centerpost — in the app, on our website, or in any of our materials — should be read as a claim to treat a medical condition. If you ever see something in Centerpost that reads that way, please tell us at medicjth@gmail.com so we can fix it.

5. Physical activity

Centerpost includes workout and gym routines. Consult a physician before starting any exercise programme, particularly if you have a heart condition, injury, are pregnant, or have any medical condition affected by exercise. Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath, and seek medical attention.

You use exercise content entirely at your own risk.

6. AI features

The AI assistant (Axis) is not a healthcare provider, therapist, or counsellor, and it cannot assess or respond to a crisis. Its answers may be wrong. Never rely on it for anything health-related. See the AI Transparency Statement.

7. Please seek professional help if

  • You are thinking about harming yourself or someone else — contact a crisis line now (top of this page)
  • Your mood, anxiety, or focus is interfering with work, relationships, or daily life
  • You are using substances in a way that worries you
  • Symptoms are getting worse, or not getting better
  • You have any question about medication
  • Any part of you is wondering whether you should talk to someone. That wondering is usually the answer.

Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you read or used in Centerpost.
