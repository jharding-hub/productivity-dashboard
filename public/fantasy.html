How picking works

Click a row (or type a name and press Enter) to give that player to the team on the clock. The pick advances automatically. Click the team name in a row's menu to assign to a different team if you fell behind. Undo removes the last pick.

bio on a row opens that player's bio, strengths, weaknesses, and team situation right over the row (quick, about 10 s) with a Draft button. Click anywhere else or press Esc to close.

Cons = average of FantasyPros ECR and ESPN rank. T = SB Nation positional tier. $ = ESPN auction value. Live ranks pulls FantasyPros half-PPR data today; the free API key returns 10 rows per call, so you get the top 10 at each position (live tier and rank shown next to the name), the top 10 overall, and injury flags.
