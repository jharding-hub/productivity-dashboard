Centerpost Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0 Effective date: August 7, 2026 Contact: medicjth@gmail.com

This is a separate, standalone policy required by the Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and Nevada SB 370. It must be linked directly from centerpost.app's homepage, not buried inside the main Privacy Policy.

It applies to everyone. Washington and Nevada residents have specific statutory rights described in §6; we extend the same practices to all users because maintaining two standards would be worse for everyone.

The short version

  • Centerpost's wellness features create data about your mental and physical state. We treat that as consumer health data.
  • These features are off until you turn them on. We ask before we collect any of it.
  • We have never sold consumer health data, and we will not. Selling it would require your separate written authorisation, which we will not seek.
  • We never share it for advertising. There is no advertising in Centerpost.
  • We do not use geofencing around health facilities, or anywhere else.
  • Your journal is encrypted with a key we don't have.
  • You can withdraw consent, or delete everything, from inside the app.

Centerpost LLC, an Indiana limited liability company. Contact: medicjth@gmail.com, 444 Valley Lane North Dr, Greenwood, IN 46142.

Centerpost LLC is the regulated entity here. We have no affiliates. There is no parent company and no corporate group with access to your data.

2. What counts as consumer health data in Centerpost

Washington law defines consumer health data broadly: information linkable to you that identifies your past, present, or future physical or mental health status — including bodily functions, mental or behavioural health interventions, and inferences drawn from any of it.

By that definition, the following are consumer health data:

Category What we collect Where it comes from
Urge logs The category you chose (impulse buy, phone/social media, snack, skip a task, or other), an optional free-text note, the length of pause you chose, and whether the urge passed or you acted on it Entered by you in the Urge Log feature
HALT+ / Sensory check-ins Which of hunger, anger, loneliness, tiredness, and sensory needs you marked as present, and how many you addressed Entered by you in the HALT+ check-in
Breathwork sessions Which breathing technique you completed and how many cycles Recorded when you finish a session
Grounding and body-scan sessions Which technique you completed Recorded when you finish a session
Mood and energy log Your energy level and mental state for a given day Entered by you
Inferences from mood and energy The suggestion Centerpost displays in response to your energy and state — this is an inference about your mental state, and we classify it as health data even though it is only a stored suggestion Generated by Centerpost from what you entered
Journal entries Whatever you write. Encrypted on your device before we receive it — see §5 Written by you
Apple Health Mindful Minutes Duration of a completed breathwork session Written by Centerpost to your device's Apple Health store. We do not receive it back, and we cannot read anything else in Apple Health.
AI messages you choose to make health-related Whatever you type into Axis Entered by you — see §4

Family features. The children's chore tracker can include a medication-reminder item. Where a parent enters that, we treat it as consumer health data belonging to the parent's account, subject to everything in this policy.

What is not consumer health data

Your tasks, projects, notes, reminders, calendar blocks, points, streaks, and settings are ordinary productivity content, covered by the main Privacy Policy. Gym and workout routines record exercise selections you make, not measurements about your body, and we do not use them to infer anything about your health.

3. Why we collect it, and nothing else

We collect consumer health data for exactly one purpose: to provide the wellness features you asked for. Concretely, that means:

  • Showing you your own history, so a tool has memory rather than resetting each time you open it
  • Displaying simple counts and trends of your own entries back to you
  • Letting the pause timer in the Urge Log run and complete

We do not:

  • Sell it, or share it in exchange for anything of value
  • Use it for advertising, marketing, or any targeting
  • Use it to build a profile of you
  • Use it to train any AI model
  • Use it to make decisions about you
  • Share it with data brokers, analytics companies, insurers, or employers
  • Use geofencing of any kind, anywhere, for any purpose

4. Who can access it

Processors. Only two of our service providers touch consumer health data, and only to store or transmit it on our instructions:

Provider What they hold Purpose
Google (Firebase / Firestore) Your check-ins, mood log, urge logs, and encrypted journal Database storage
Cloudflare Data in transit; short-lived security counters Hosting and delivery

Both are contractually restricted to processing on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes.

The AI provider (Anthropic). Centerpost does not attach your journal, mood log, check-ins, or urge logs to any AI request. Those never form part of a prompt. We have verified this in our own source code.

However, Axis is a free-text box. If you type health-related content into it, that text goes to Anthropic with your message. This is under your control, and we tell you so before your first AI interaction. Anthropic's commercial terms provide that API content is not used to train their models (as of August 7, 2026).

Human access. No employee, contractor, or operator of Centerpost routinely reads your health data. There is no support tool that surfaces it and no analytics dashboard that displays it. The only circumstances in which a human could access it are:

  1. A specific, documented technical investigation you have asked us to carry out
  2. Where the law compels us — in which case we will notify you unless legally prohibited

We do not read your journal in any circumstance: it is encrypted with a key derived from your PIN, which we do not have.

Abuse detection does not read your messages. If a message you send to Axis matches a known prompt-injection pattern, we record which pattern matched, your account ID, and the time — not the text you wrote. That is the only automated inspection of AI input we perform, and no prose reaches us or any operator.

We have never received a government demand for consumer health data. If that changes, we will challenge overbroad requests and notify affected users where legally permitted.

5. How it is protected

Everything in §7 of the main Privacy Policy applies. Specific to health data:

  • Your journal is encrypted on your device with AES-GCM-256, under a key derived from your PIN using PBKDF2 with 310,000 iterations and a random per-user salt. Your PIN is never stored or sent to us. We cannot decrypt your journal. On iPhone, you can have the device Keychain hold that key behind Face ID.
  • Database rules restrict health data to your own account. No other user can read it.
  • All transmission is over HTTPS.
  • Check-in history is capped at the 500 most recent entries; older ones are dropped automatically.

One thing to be aware of: check-ins and mood entries are also cached in your device's local storage so the app works offline. That local cache is not separately encrypted by Centerpost — it relies on your device's own protection. Your journal cache is ciphertext and stays encrypted. If you share a device, use your operating system's account separation.

6. Your rights

Under the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370, and as a matter of our own practice for everyone else, you have the right to:

  • Know and access — get a list of the consumer health data we hold about you, including who we have shared it with, and a copy of the data itself
  • Withdraw consent — for collection, for sharing, or for both, at any time
  • Delete — have your consumer health data deleted from our systems, including from our processors' systems

Washington requires deletion requests to be honoured and passed to all downstream recipients. We do that. Because we do not sell or share your health data, there is no chain of recipients to notify beyond the two processors named in §4.

Exercising them

Right Fastest route
Delete everything In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your health data along with the rest of your account. Immediate; no request needed.
Delete just health data Turn off wellness features in Settings, then delete individual entries. Or email us to request deletion of the health documents only, keeping your account.
Withdraw consent In the app: Settings → Wellness features → off. Collection stops immediately.
Access / copy In the app: Settings → Export (includes a decrypted journal copy). Or email us.

By email: medicjth@gmail.com. We respond within 45 days, extendable once by 45 days where the law permits, with an explanation. We verify by confirming control of your account email.

If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying with "Appeal." We will respond in writing within 45 days. If we deny the appeal, you may complain to the Washington State Attorney General at https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint, or your own state's Attorney General.

A note on MHMDA's private right of action. Washington's law is enforceable by consumers directly under the Consumer Protection Act. We would rather fix a problem than litigate one — if something here doesn't match your experience of the app, tell us and we will correct it.

7. Sale of consumer health data

We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not intend to.

Washington law requires a specific, separate, signed authorisation before any sale — distinct from consent, with mandated contents and a right to revoke. We have never sought such an authorisation and have no plans to. If that ever changed, it would require your explicit signed authorisation, obtained separately, which you could revoke at any time, and we would notify you before asking.

We also do not:

  • Share health data with data brokers
  • Exchange it for anything of value
  • Provide it to advertisers, insurers, or employers
  • Use geofencing around health-care facilities or any other location

8. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be notified in the app and by email before taking effect. We will not apply a materially different use to health data already collected without asking for your consent again.
